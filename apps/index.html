Free file tools for Windows

Each tool runs on your PC, in the browser or as a Microsoft Store app. Your files are never uploaded.

  • Docpeek

    Open Apple Pages, Numbers and Keynote files on Windows, and save them as Word, Excel or PowerPoint.

    Use in your browser · Microsoft Store

  • Redate

    Put the real dates back on photos and videos from a Google Photos (Google Takeout) download.

    Use in your browser · Microsoft Store

  • Clipready

    Turn iPhone videos (MOV) into MP4 with no quality loss, and voice memos or video sound into MP3.

    Use in your browser · Microsoft Store

  • Paperlock

    Add or remove a PDF password (AES-256).

    Use in your browser

  • Pagestack

    Merge PDF files and split them into pages or ranges.

    Use in your browser

  • Mailpeek

    Open and search .mbox mail files from Gmail (Google Takeout), Apple Mail or Thunderbird.

    Use in your browser

  • Tidyroll

    Bring iPhone photos to Windows: HEIC to JPG, sorted by date.

    Use in your browser
